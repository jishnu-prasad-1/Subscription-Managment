## Brand & Style

This design system blends the unapologetic energy and visual tactility of 1990s personal computing with high-throughput modern software ergonomics. It delivers an audacious counterweight to sterile enterprise tools by fusing neo-brutalist construction, saturated chromatic pops, stacked window frames, and electric retro accents.

The design movement combines **Neo-Brutalism** and **90s Retro-Futurism**:
- Heavy architectural ink borders and crisp 3px/4px hard offset shadows create immediate physical depth without blurred atmospheric fog.
- Saturated electric highlights—mint turquoise, chrome yellow, watermelon pink, and vibrant electric orange—create high-voltage visual hierarchy against warm, creamy paper canvases.
- Windows feature physical title strips, mechanical control icons, and isometric accent geometries (lightning bolts, cassette tabs, grid backdrops) designed for data-rich SaaS dashboards that demand joy, clarity, and bold distinction.

## Colors

The color palette leverages high-saturation 90s chromatic tones calibrated against structural charcoal ink and luminous cream backgrounds:

- **Primary (`#00D2B4` / `#38EFD0`)**: Electric Mint Turquoise anchors the application's interactive core. It commands key CTAs, active window headers, focus states, and foundational SaaS telemetry badges.
- **Secondary (`#FFD600` / `#FFC000`)**: Sunny Chrome Yellow drives high-visibility callouts, warning flags, promotional pills, and converted metrics.
- **Tertiary (`#FF5376` / `#FF6B8B`)**: Hot Watermelon Pink provides high-contrast energy for destructive alerts, churn risks, priority tags, and punchy promotional badges.
- **Electric Orange (`#FF6B00` / `#FF8533`)**: High-voltage accent for secondary notifications, trial countdowns, and active progression milestones.
- **Deep Ink Charcoal (`#1E1E24` / `#222228`)**: Replaces standard system blacks to delineate sharp strokes, heavy offset drop shadows, and high-impact structural typography.
- **Cream Canvases (`#FFF8EE` / `#FFF0DC`)**: Imbues viewports with warm vintage tactile paper aesthetics, eliminating sterile digital white while providing ample contrast against pure white card surfaces (`#FFFFFF`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs mechanical punch with effortless legibility:

- **Display & Headings (`Space Grotesk`)**: Geometric, sharp, and confident. Heavy weights (`700`) paired with subtle negative tracking create an unmistakable editorial presence reminiscent of 90s computing magazines and retro software packaging.
- **Body & Continuous Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Neutralized and rounded, balancing geometric clarity with comfortable reading over dense SaaS analytics tables, documentation, and customer communications.
- **Data Tickers, Labels & UI Badges (`Space Grotesk`)**: Capitalized tracking across micro-labels (`label-sm`, `label-md`) delivers an unmistakable hardware terminal aesthetic suitable for financial metrics, KPI cards, and subscription state pills.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is anchored by a structured 12-column grid system over a maximum container width of 1360px:

- **Grid Canvas**: The main viewport background `#FFF8EE` optionally incorporates an architectural 1px hairline grid pattern (`#1E1E24` at 5% opacity, 24px step) to reference 90s desktop workspace backgrounds.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - **Mobile (< 640px)**: 4 columns, `margin-mobile` (1rem / 16px), stacked single-column window containers, docked compact bottom navigation.
  - **Tablet (640px – 1024px)**: 8 columns, `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px), split modular panels and compressed sidebars.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12 columns, `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px), persistent utility sidebar dock, and multi-tier floating window widgets.
- **Modular Rhythm**: Padding adheres strictly to a 4px/8px baseline grid. Component containers balance dense tabular interfaces with comfortable breathing room using `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects blurry gaussian drop shadows in favor of tangible neo-brutalist mechanical relief:

- **Structural Outlines**: All interactive buttons, inputs, modals, popovers, and cards feature an unapologetic `2px solid #1E1E24` stroke. Micro-elements (nested chips and checkboxes) utilize a tight `1.5px solid #1E1E24` stroke.
- **Hard Offset Shadows**:
  - **Resting Surface (Cards, Panels)**: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #1E1E24`
  - **Interactive Element Default (Buttons, Chips)**: `box-shadow: 3px 3px 0px #1E1E24`
  - **Elevated / Floating Modals & Tooltips**: `box-shadow: 6px 6px 0px #1E1E24`
  - **Active / Pressed State**: `transform: translate(3px, 3px); box-shadow: 0px 0px 0px #1E1E24`
- **Stacked Window Metaphor**: Overlapping panels utilize offset layering (e.g., repeating window frames shifted by -6px X and -6px Y) with solid ink borders, directly evoking 90s multi-tasking GUIs.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a deliberate neo-brutalist construction with soft tactile corners:

- **Cards, Panels & Windows**: Standardize on `roundedness: 1` (`4px` / `0.25rem` up to `6px` / `0.375rem`), preserving tight, boxy hardware contours while avoiding harsh knife-edge points.
- **Status Pills & Action Tags**: Employ full pill rounds (`9999px`) to create an immediate shape contrast against rigid rectangular windows and data tables.
- **Window Title Strips & Decorative Caps**: Maintain square internal corners (`0px`) where segmented header bars join their parent container walls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#00D2B4`, text `#1E1E24`, border `2px solid #1E1E24`, shadow `3px 3px 0px #1E1E24`. Typography `label-lg` uppercase. Hover brightens to `#38EFD0`. Active state applies `transform: translate(3px, 3px)` with `box-shadow: 0px 0px 0px #1E1E24`.
- **Secondary**: Background `#FFD600`, text `#1E1E24`, border `2px solid #1E1E24`, shadow `3px 3px 0px #1E1E24`. Hover transitions to `#FFC000`.
- **Tertiary / Destructive**: Background `#FF5376`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `2px solid #1E1E24`, shadow `3px 3px 0px #1E1E24`.
- **Ghost / Outlined**: Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1E1E24`, border `2px solid #1E1E24`, shadow `3px 3px 0px #1E1E24`.

### Retro Window Cards & Panels
- White `#FFFFFF` core surface enclosed in `2px solid #1E1E24` with a `4px 4px 0px #1E1E24` hard shadow.
- Top section features a dedicated 36px title strip filled with `#FFF0DC` or `#00D2B4`, separated from card contents by a `2px solid #1E1E24` border.
- The title strip contains an uppercase `Space Grotesk` title on the left and 3 retro square control icons on the right (`_`, `□`, `✕`) housed in 16×16px bordered squares.

### Chips & Badges
- **Capsule Badges**: Border `1.5px solid #1E1E24`, radius `9999px`, font `label-sm` uppercase.
  - Active / Growth: `#00D2B4` background, `#1E1E24` text.
  - Pending / Warning: `#FFD600` background, `#1E1E24` text.
  - Critical / Churn: `#FF5376` background, `#FFFFFF` text.
  - Beta / New Feature: `#FF6B00` background, `#FFFFFF` text.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- Crisp `#FFFFFF` background, `2px solid #1E1E24` border, `4px` radius.
- Padding: 10px 14px. Default shadow: inset `1px 1px 0px rgba(30, 30, 36, 0.1)`.
- Focus state: Outline none, `box-shadow: 3px 3px 0px #1E1E24`, border maintains `#1E1E24`. Placeholder styled in `#1E1E24` with 45% opacity.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Checkbox**: 20×20px square, `4px` radius, `2px solid #1E1E24`, background `#FFFFFF`. Selected state fills with `#00D2B4` with a bold `#1E1E24` check mark and `1px 1px 0px #1E1E24` shadow.
- **Radio**: 20×20px circle, `2px solid #1E1E24`, background `#FFFFFF`. Selected state contains an inner solid circular dot of `#1E1E24` (8px diameter).

### Tables & Data Grids
- Outer wrapper is enclosed in a `2px solid #1E1E24` border with `4px 4px 0px #1E1E24` shadow.
- Table headers utilize `#FFF0DC` background, `2px solid #1E1E24` bottom border, and uppercase bold `label-md` headers.
- Alternating rows transition from `#FFFFFF` to `#FFF8EE`. Row hover initiates an instant background flash of `#38EFD0` at 25% opacity with a solid `1.5px solid #1E1E24` row focus outline.

### Segmented Metric Gauges
- Progress indicators mimic vintage 90s LED bars: enclosed in a `2px solid #1E1E24` outer pill, filled with segmented discrete tick marks in alternating `#00D2B4` and `#FFD600`.
